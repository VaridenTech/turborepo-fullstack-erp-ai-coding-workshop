Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

Manager stock adjustments and the audit-record foundation that later events reuse.

- Manager-only stock increase/decrease with a mandatory reason, recorded as an ADJUSTMENT movement in history.
- Reject a reduction greater than the available balance.
- Establish the `AuditLog` record (actor, timestamp, source document, outcome) that PO approvals, goods receipts, and sales confirmations will reuse. Audit reads are permission-restricted.

`Covers:` INV-05, INV-03 (non-negative), NFR-03 (audit foundation).
`Demo:` A Manager adjusts stock up and down with a reason and sees both the movement history entry and the audit record; a reduction larger than the balance is rejected.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test), 4 (Postgres integration).

## Acceptance criteria

- A Manager increase/decrease with a reason appends an ADJUSTMENT movement and an AuditLog row (actor, timestamp, source document, outcome), observable at seam 4. (Fails today: no adjustment route or AuditLog exist.)
- An adjustment without a reason is rejected by the Zod schema (seam 2) and the API (seam 1).
- A reduction greater than the available balance is rejected and leaves stock unchanged, observable at seam 4.
- A non-Manager adjustment request is rejected at the API, observable at seam 1.
- Audit reads are rejected for roles without permission, observable at seam 1.
