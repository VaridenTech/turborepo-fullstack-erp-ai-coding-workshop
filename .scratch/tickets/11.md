Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

The daily sales report with correct Asia/Bangkok day boundaries.

- RPT-01 daily sales report filtered by date range and store; include only successfully confirmed sales; show document count, units sold, and total sales amount.
- A reporting day runs from 00:00:00 inclusive to the next day 00:00:00 exclusive in Asia/Bangkok, computed from the sale confirmation timestamp (timestamps stored in UTC).
- Show zero totals and a clear empty-state message when no records match. Monetary amounts to two decimal places. Lists paginate and order newest-first.

`Covers:` RPT-01, Section 6.2 (timezone, empty state, pagination).
`Demo:` A Manager views a daily sales report for B01 over a date range and sees document count, units, and total; a day with no sales shows zero totals and an empty-state message.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test), 4 (Postgres integration with boundary fixtures).

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-08 (RPT-01 half): when no sales exist on the selected day, sales totals are zero and a clear empty state is shown. Observable at seams 3 and 4.
- The report counts only confirmed sales and computes count, units, and total to two decimal places, observable at seam 4.
- A sale confirmed just before Asia/Bangkok midnight falls in that day; one just after falls in the next day, proven by boundary fixtures at seam 4. (Fails today: no sales report or timezone windowing exists.)
- The report applies the same store permission as the screen (store employees see only their store), observable at seam 1.
