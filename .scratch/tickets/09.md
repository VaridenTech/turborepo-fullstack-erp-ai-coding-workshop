Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

Goods receipts: partial receiving that increases stock, with idempotent confirmation and concurrency safety. Establishes the idempotency-key handling later tickets reuse.

- `GoodsReceipt` + `GoodsReceiptLine`, each carrying the client-supplied `Idempotency-Key` header, unique per endpoint and actor.
- Store employees receive only Approved or Partially Received orders for their assigned store. The receiving screen shows ordered, previously received, and outstanding quantities per line.
- Partial receipts across deliveries; each received quantity is a non-negative whole number with at least one line greater than zero; cumulative receipts cannot exceed ordered; products outside the order cannot be added.
- Confirmation creates a goods receipt number, increases destination-store stock, records inventory movements in one transaction, and flips the order to Partially Received (remaining outstanding) or Completed (all received).
- A replay with the same idempotency key returns the original receipt; a new key creates a new receipt. Concurrent receiving requests cannot exceed the ordered quantity in total. Confirmed receipts are viewable but not editable or cancellable.
- A product deactivated after approval cannot be received against that order (Q-02).

`Covers:` GR-01, GR-02, GR-03, GR-04, GR-05, Q-02.
`Demo:` staff_b01 receives part of PO-DEMO-001, sees outstanding update and the order move to Partially Received; a replayed confirm returns the original receipt; an over-receipt is rejected.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test), 4 (Postgres integration).

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-01 (GR-01 to GR-03): staff_b01 receives 8 packs of SKU001 against PO-DEMO-001; B01 stock becomes 18, outstanding is 12, status Partially Received, and exactly one receipt and one receipt movement exist. Observable at seam 4.
- AC-02 (GR-04): after successfully receiving 8, resending the same request returns the original receipt; stock stays 18 and cumulative received stays 8. Observable at seam 4.
- AC-03 (GR-02, GR-05): attempting to receive 21 is rejected; no receipt or movement is created; stock stays 10 and status stays Approved. Observable at seam 4.
- AC-04 (GR-01, NFR-01): a direct API request by staff_b02 to receive goods for B01 is rejected and no business data changes. Observable at seams 1 and 4.
- A receipt line referencing a product outside the order, or a product deactivated after approval, is rejected, observable at seam 1.
- Two concurrent receiving requests cannot push cumulative received above the ordered quantity, observable at seam 4.
