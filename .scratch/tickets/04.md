Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

Products master data with SKU rules, deactivation, and per-store reorder thresholds.

- `Product` model: SKU, name, category, unit, selling price, active status, and a per-store reorder threshold.
- SKU unique across the organization and immutable once a transaction references the product.
- Reject negative selling price and negative reorder threshold; constrain stock-related quantities to whole numbers.
- Deactivate a product while keeping it searchable in historical records; block inactive products from new documents.

`Covers:` MD-01, MD-02.
`Demo:` An Administrator creates SKU001-003 with the Section 8.1 prices, deactivates one, confirms it stays searchable in history but cannot be added to a new document.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test).

## Acceptance criteria

- Creating a product with a unique SKU persists it; a duplicate SKU is rejected with the Thai error envelope, observable at seam 1. (Fails today: no Product model or routes exist.)
- Negative selling price or negative reorder threshold is rejected by the Zod schema (seam 2) and the API (seam 1).
- A per-store reorder threshold can be set for each store, observable at seam 1.
- A deactivated product remains returned by historical search but is rejected when added to a new document, observable at seam 1.
- SKU cannot be changed once referenced by a transaction, observable at seam 1.
