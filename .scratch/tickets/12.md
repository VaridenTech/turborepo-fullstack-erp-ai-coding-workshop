Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

Stock-on-hand and low-stock reports.

- RPT-02 stock-on-hand and low-stock reports by store, with product search.
- Zero-stock products remain visible when they match the filters.
- A product is low-stock when its balance is at or below the per-store reorder threshold.

`Covers:` RPT-02.
`Demo:` A Manager views B01 stock-on-hand and low-stock reports; SKU003 at balance 0 appears in the low-stock report because it is at or below its threshold of 3.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test), 4 (Postgres integration).

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-08 (RPT-02 half): SKU003 appears in the B01 low-stock report because its balance of 0 is at or below the threshold of 3. Observable at seams 3 and 4. (Fails today: no stock report exists.)
- A zero-stock product that matches the search filter remains visible in the stock-on-hand report, observable at seam 4.
- Low-stock is computed as balance <= per-store reorder threshold, observable at seam 4.
- The report applies the same store permission as the screen, observable at seam 1.
