Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

Purchase order drafts: creation, editing, and line validation.

- `PurchaseOrder` + `PurchaseOrderLine` models.
- Create a draft with a supplier, destination store, expected receipt date, and at least one product line.
- Ordered quantities are positive whole numbers; purchase unit prices are non-negative; duplicate SKUs within one order are prohibited.
- Drafts can be edited.

`Covers:` PO-01.
`Demo:` A Purchasing officer creates and edits a draft purchase order against SUP01 with B01 as the destination store.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test).

## Acceptance criteria

- Creating a draft with a supplier, destination store, expected date, and at least one valid line persists it in Draft status. (Fails today: no PurchaseOrder model or routes exist.)
- A line with a non-positive or non-whole quantity, a negative price, or a duplicate SKU is rejected by the Zod schema (seam 2) and the API (seam 1).
- A draft with zero lines is rejected, observable at seam 1.
- A draft can be edited while in Draft, observable at seam 1.
- Only a Purchasing officer can create/edit drafts; other roles are rejected at the API, observable at seam 1.
