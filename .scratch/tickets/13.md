Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

The stock movement history report, store-scoped.

- RPT-03 movement history filtered by product, store, movement type, and date range, with access to the source document.
- Store employees see their assigned store only; Managers see all stores.

`Covers:` RPT-03.
`Demo:` A Manager filters movement history across stores by type and date and opens a source receipt or sale; staff_b01 sees only B01 movements.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test), 4 (Postgres integration).

## Acceptance criteria

- Movement history filters by product, store, type, and date range and links to the source document, observable at seam 4. (Fails today: no movement history report exists.)
- A Store employee's movement history is restricted to their assigned store; a Manager's spans all stores, observable at seams 1 and 4.
- Results paginate and order newest-first, observable at seam 1.
