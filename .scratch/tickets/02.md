Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

The four-role model with per-user store assignment, plus the seeded users the acceptance scenarios need.

- Replace the `Role` enum (`ADMIN`, `STAFF`) with `ADMINISTRATOR`, `PURCHASING_OFFICER`, `STORE_EMPLOYEE`, `MANAGER`.
- Add a single store assignment to users (`storeId`): required for Store employees, null for the others.
- A reusable store-scope enforcement helper for handlers: Store employees are restricted to their assigned store on every read and write; Managers and Administrators are not store-scoped (Managers see all stores).
- Seed users from the TOR Section 8.1: buyer01 (purchasing), staff_b01 (store B01), staff_b02 (store B02), manager01 (manager), plus an administrator.

`Covers:` Section 2 (roles and permissions), NFR-01 (role and store permissions enforced at the API).
`Demo:` Each seeded user signs in; a store employee's requests are scoped to their assigned store; an Administrator can manage master data but has no transaction-approval rights.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 3 (web component test).

## Acceptance criteria

- The four roles exist and the seed produces buyer01, staff_b01, staff_b02, manager01, and an administrator, each able to sign in. (Fails today: only ADMIN/STAFF exist and seed users are admin@/staff@.)
- A Store employee calling a store-scoped route for a store other than their assigned one is rejected at the API, observable at seam 1.
- A Manager calling the same route across any store is allowed, observable at seam 1.
- An Administrator has no approval capability wired to their role, observable at seam 1.
