Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

The purchase order approval workflow and state machine.

Statuses: Draft, Pending Approval, Approved, Partially Received, Completed, Rejected, Cancelled. Legal transitions (from the agreed prototype), and no others:

```
Draft            --submit-->  Pending Approval        (purchasing officer)
Pending Approval --approve--> Approved                (manager, reason)
Pending Approval --reject-->  Rejected                (manager, reason)
Draft            --cancel-->  Cancelled               (creator or manager, reason)
Pending Approval --cancel-->  Cancelled               (creator or manager, reason)
```

(The receive-driven transitions to Partially Received/Completed are implemented by the goods-receipt ticket.)

- Creating or approving an order never changes stock.
- Approved lines, quantities, prices, and destination store are immutable.
- Rejected orders remain viewable; resubmission requires a new order.
- Cancelling Approved or Partially Received orders is out of scope.
- Approval and rejection outcomes are written to the audit log.

`Covers:` PO-02, PO-03, PO-04, NFR-03 (approval audit).
`Demo:` A Purchasing officer submits a draft; a Manager approves or rejects it with a reason; an illegal transition is rejected with a Thai error; an Approved order cannot be edited.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test).

## Acceptance criteria

- Submit moves Draft to Pending Approval; approve/reject (with reason) moves Pending Approval to Approved/Rejected and writes an audit row, observable at seam 1. (Fails today: no status transitions exist.)
- Approving or creating an order makes no stock movement, observable at seam 1.
- An Approved order rejects any edit to lines, quantities, prices, or destination, observable at seam 1.
- Any illegal transition (e.g. cancel an Approved order, approve a Draft) is rejected with the Thai error envelope, observable at seam 1.
- Approve/reject without a reason is rejected by the Zod schema (seam 2) and the API (seam 1).
- A rejected order stays viewable, observable at seam 1.
