Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

The NFR-05 performance load test and supporting indexes.

- A committed, scripted load test against an agreed test environment: 1,000 products, 10,000 movements, and 10 simulated concurrent users, over a continuous 5-minute run.
- Target the search and stock-view APIs; CSV exports are excluded.
- Add the database indexes needed to meet the target: SKU, product name, store, and movement timestamp.

`Covers:` NFR-05.
`Demo:` A load-test report showing p95 response times of no more than 2 seconds for the search and stock-view APIs over the 5-minute run.
`Seams:` 4 (Postgres integration), plus a dedicated load-test harness.

## Acceptance criteria

- With 1,000 products, 10,000 movements, and 10 concurrent users over 5 minutes, the search and stock-view APIs achieve p95 <= 2 seconds, demonstrated by the load-test report. (Fails today: no load test, seed at scale, or supporting indexes exist.)
- The supporting indexes (SKU, product name, store, movement timestamp) exist and are exercised by the tested queries.
- The load test is runnable as a committed script in the agreed environment.
