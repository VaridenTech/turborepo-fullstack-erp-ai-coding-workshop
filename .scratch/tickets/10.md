Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

Sales recording and confirmation, atomic and idempotent, with concurrency safety.

- `Sale` + `SaleLine`, each carrying the client-supplied `Idempotency-Key`.
- Store employees create sales for their assigned store using active products and positive whole-number quantities. The current selling price is applied and retained in the document; users cannot override prices.
- Draft sales do not reduce stock. On confirmation, validate the latest stock for every line; if any product has insufficient stock, reject the entire document and identify the affected products. Partial posting is prohibited.
- Successful confirmation generates a document number, records the sale, and decreases stock with movement history in one transaction. Duplicate confirmation (same key) creates no second sale or deduction.
- Concurrent confirmations on the same stock cannot oversell (INV-04).
- Confirmed sales are viewable but not editable, cancellable, or returnable.

`Covers:` SAL-01, SAL-02, SAL-03, SAL-04, INV-04.
`Demo:` staff_b01 sells SKU001 and SKU002, sees the total and the reduced balances; a replayed confirm changes nothing; a sale exceeding stock is rejected identifying the product.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 3 (web component test), 4 (Postgres integration).

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-05 (SAL-02): selling 11 packs of SKU001 when opening stock is 10 fails; no confirmed sale or stock deduction is created, and the response identifies SKU001. Observable at seam 4.
- AC-06 (SAL-01 to SAL-03): selling 2 packs of SKU001 and 1 pack of SKU002 totals THB 155.00; balances become 8 and 4; resending the same request causes no further change. Observable at seam 4.
- AC-07 (INV-04): with SKU001 stock at 10, two concurrent sales of 7 each result in exactly one success, one rejection, and final stock of 3. Observable at seam 4.
- A price-override attempt or a non-whole/non-positive quantity is rejected by the Zod schema (seam 2) and the API (seam 1).
- An inactive product cannot be sold, observable at seam 1.
- A confirmed sale rejects edit/cancel/return attempts, observable at seam 1.
