Part of the Retail ERP first-release spec (#1).

## What to build

CSV export for sales and stock reports, Thai-safe and injection-safe.

- RPT-04 CSV export for sales and stock reports.
- Apply the same filters and permissions as the on-screen report.
- Encode UTF-8 with a BOM so spreadsheet software renders Thai text correctly.
- Neutralize formula injection: any cell beginning with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab, or carriage return is prefixed so a spreadsheet does not evaluate it.

`Covers:` RPT-04.
`Demo:` A Manager exports a Thai sales report that opens correctly in a spreadsheet; a cell whose value starts with `=` is neutralized.
`Seams:` 1 (API controller spec), 2 (Zod schema test), 4 (Postgres integration).

## Acceptance criteria

- The CSV export is UTF-8 with a BOM and renders Thai text, observable at seams 1 and 4. (Fails today: no export route exists.)
- A cell value starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, tab, or carriage return is neutralized in the output, observable at seam 1.
- The export applies the same filters and permissions as the screen (store employees cannot export another store's data), observable at seam 1.
